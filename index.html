bot ops

A private operations tool for authorized staff. Its Google connection lets an operator review operational workbooks from their own Google Drive and Google Sheets account.

The Google integration requests read-only access to Drive files and spreadsheets. It does not request access to Gmail messages and cannot edit or delete Google files using these permissions.

Google authorization is performed by the account owner. These public information pages contain no workbook data, account credentials, or bot source code.

For support, contact the operator who provided access to this tool. Operators can also contact the bot through Operasional Pusat on Telegram.

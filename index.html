bot ops

A private operations tool for authorized staff. Its Google connection lets an operator review operational workbooks from their own Google Drive and Google Sheets account and refresh configured workbook input data for balance checks.

The Google integration requests read-only access to Drive files and, when the account owner enables workbook filling, edit access to Google Sheets. The tool limits updates to configured workbook input areas used for operational balance checks. Opening balances, expense-status choices, and existing calculation rules are preserved. It does not request access to Gmail messages or perform financial transfers.

Google authorization is performed by the account owner. These public information pages contain no workbook data, account credentials, or bot source code.

For support, contact the operator who provided access to this tool. Operators can also contact the bot through Operasional Pusat on Telegram.

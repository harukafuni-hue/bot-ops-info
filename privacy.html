Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026. This policy covers the Google Drive and Google Sheets connection used by bot ops.

Access and purpose

After the Google account owner authorizes access, the tool can read Drive file metadata and contents, including spreadsheets. Although the requested read-only permissions can cover all files available to that account, the current connection is intended for operator-selected workbook review. No Gmail message permission is requested.

Storage and processing

Google data is retrieved to the operator-managed server for the requested review. Downloaded workbooks or extracted review material may remain there until the operator deletes them. OAuth credentials are stored separately from source code with restricted filesystem permissions.

Sharing

The public information site does not display Google data. Review material may be provided to an assistant or processing service at the operator's direction to carry out the requested review. The operator is responsible for selecting the recipients and applicable access and retention settings. This integration is not intended to sell Google data or use it for advertising.

Control and deletion

The account owner can revoke Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized reads; it does not remove files already stored on the operator's server. Contact the operator to request deletion of stored credentials, downloaded files, or review material.

Contact

Contact the operator who supplied access to bot ops, including through Operasional Pusat on Telegram.
